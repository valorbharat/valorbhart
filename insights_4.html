NSIC — Single Point Registration

National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. (NSIC) is a Government of India, Mini Ratna PSU under the Ministry of MSME, established in 1955. Its Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS) gives registered MSEs:

  • Tender sets free of cost
  • Exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
  • Price preference up to L1+15% on a portion of the order
  • Access to the 25% annual MSE procurement reservation
